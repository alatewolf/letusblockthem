# Let Us Block Them: privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Let Us Block Them is a Chrome extension. It hides, on your own screen, the members of an FL group you choose.

## What it reads

- When you press Mass Hide Members, and at most once a day after that, it reads the group's member list pages on FL. It reads them with your own browser session, the same way you would by clicking through the pages.
- On the FL pages you open, it reads the usernames on posts, comments, notifications and messages, only to decide what to hide.

## What it stores

- The groups you hide and their members' usernames. These stay in your browser's extension storage on your own device.

## What it sends

- Nothing. The extension has no server and no analytics. It sends no data to the developer or to anyone else. Its only network requests are the member list page reads on FL itself.

## Sale and sharing

- The developer does not sell, share or transfer any data, because the developer never receives any.

## Deleting your data

- Open a hidden group and press "Show this group's members" to delete that group's list.
- Remove the extension to delete everything it stored.
