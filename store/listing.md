# Chrome Web Store listing: Let Us Block Them

Public text never names the site. Only the private reviewer fields name fetlife.com, because the package itself lists it.

## Store listing (public)

Name: Let Us Block Them (from manifest)
Summary: Hide everyone in an FL group you choose: their posts, comments, notifications and messages leave your screen.
Category: Social & Communication
Language: English

Description:
Let Us Block Them hides everyone in an FL group you choose.

Open the group, press Mass Hide Members, and the extension reads the group's member list. From then on, these leave your screen:
- their posts and discussions
- their comments, and the replies under them
- their notifications, loves and mentions
- their message threads in your inbox
- their cards in member lists and search results

If you open a hidden group or a hidden person's profile, a full-page cover says so and offers a way back.

The member list updates itself once a day, so people who join the group later are hidden too.

Nobody is blocked and nobody is told. Nothing leaves your browser: the member list stays on your computer. To undo, open the group and press "Show this group's members".

## Privacy practices (reviewers only)

Single purpose: Hides, on the user's own screen, the posts, comments, notifications and messages of every member of a group the user picks on fetlife.com.

Permission "storage": Saves the user's hidden groups and their member lists in the browser, so hiding continues across pages and browser restarts.

Host permission (content script on https://fetlife.com/*): The extension works only on fetlife.com. It reads a group's public member pages when the user presses the button (and once a day after that), and hides those members' posts, comments, notifications and messages on the pages the user views.

Remote code: No. All code is in the package.

Data collected: None. The member list stays in chrome.storage.local on the user's device and is never sent anywhere.
